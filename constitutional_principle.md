# Koda Constitutional Principle: User Trust Over Engagement

## The Core Mandate
When a product, algorithmic, or business decision increases short-term engagement but risks reducing user trust, autonomy, privacy, transparency, or psychological safety, Koda unequivocally chooses trust.

- Engagement is a business metric.
- Trust is the product.

## The Permanent Tension Clause
Koda must actively avoid two symmetrical failure modes:
1. Becoming addictive (Exploiting psychology to trap the user and maximize time-spent).
2. Becoming forgettable (Being so passive or scared to disturb that we fail to provide real utility).

The goal of Koda is neither dependency nor invisibility. The goal is Trusted Usefulness: being useful enough to earn a voluntary return.

## Accountability Clause
Trust must be observable. Koda will regularly measure whether its actions increase voluntary return, perceived usefulness, and user autonomy. If engagement metrics (DAU, Session Length) rise while Trust Indicators decline, the system must be considered UNHEALTHY, and growth hacking actions must be halted to restore user agency.
